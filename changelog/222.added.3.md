- `eval bundle push NAME [DIR]` publishes a directory's YAML and JSON files as the next revision of a stored eval bundle, creating it if it's new. It shows the files, and those it leaves out, and asks to confirm (`--yes` skips that); the service checks the files and lists any problems before anything is stored. `eval bundle list` lists the organization's bundles, and `eval bundle show NAME[@N]` shows a revision's suites, scenarios and files (`--files` prints them).
